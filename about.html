About us

Experience in accounting. Experience in ownership.

Eagle Accounting Services is a Nashville-based firm that helps small business owners understand and trust their numbers.

Our vision

We help small business owners gain peace of mind and confidence in their finances so they can focus on running and growing their businesses.

Combining extensive accounting and financial management experience with firsthand business ownership, we bring a business-minded perspective that goes beyond the numbers to help owners make informed decisions and build stronger businesses.

We know what it's like to sign the checks, make payroll and wonder whether the numbers on the screen are right. That's why we focus on more than compliance. We want you to understand your finances well enough to act on them.

You'll work with people who know your business, reply when you reach out and explain things in plain language.

How we work

What you can expect from us

Accuracy you can rely on

Every account reconciled and every report reviewed before it reaches you.

Plain-language answers

We explain what your numbers mean and what to do about them, without the jargon.

Focus on your growth

We look for ways to improve cash flow, margins and decisions, not just record what happened.
